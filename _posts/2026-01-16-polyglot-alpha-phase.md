**Polyglot Pirates** will start the **Alpha Testing** phase on **1st of Feb**. It will be a language learning multiplayer co-op game about pirates looking for their lost treasure.

- [Registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdlQ8G2K8xzFseLxhx-rBCh65hYDMv_47sQssHWdpu5P7ELzA/viewform): If you want to register for the upcoming alpha.


## Features

In the **Alpha Version** of the game will have the following:

- **Practice mode** lets you practice without the stress of the levels (without progression) and it will work offline also.
- **Story path** to complete as you finish the arcade levels. Find out why the sea creatures stole your treasure and get it back!
- **Watch the leaderboards** to see your best score while competing with other learners around the world.
- **Read the dictionary** grouped by categories and subcategories even when offline.
- **Customize with accessories** the main player, that are unlocked by playing.

Sometime in start of march, in the **Beta Version** there will be the following features:
- **Play together** by inviting friends in the same level.
- **5000 words per language** (still work in progress). Planned at release is to have 30 languages.
