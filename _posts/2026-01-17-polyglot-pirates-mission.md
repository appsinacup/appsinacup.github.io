**Polyglot Pirates** is a **language learning** multiplayer co-op game about pirates looking for their lost treasure.

The player must guide the pirate ship towards the treasure by **matching words**, playing **hangman** and **finding words** in a square of letters, all while trying to do it as **quick as possible**. The game will both work **offline** and **online multiplayer** (together with friends, **even if** they are **learning another language**).

We are targeting to have **5000 words** in **30 languages** (at launch).

## Mission

The **mission** is to help people **learn new languages**, and motivate them to do so with **competition**. I strongly believe competition brings the best out of everyone.

## Who am I?

I am Dragos, and for the last 3 years I have contributed a lot of my free time to Open Source Software for Game Dev. I have learned a great deal by doing so, and have started developing this game with 3 friends.

## What is this game

I wanted an **alternative to Duolingo** for language learning. I really like their main gameplay loop, however I **hate their ads**, **subscription prices**, **energy system**, **AI translations**, etc. I wanted also another **European alternative**.

So, to keep this short, what I want out of this game are the following:
- **No Ads**
- **Offline Modes**: Practice mode, Dictionary view, etc.
- **Multiplayer Modes**: Competitive multiplayer modes, where the fastest wins. As well as tournaments organized in the weekends, etc.
- **Story Path**: Complete it as you finish the arcade levels. Find out why the sea creatures stole your treasure and get it back
- **Customization**: Unlock and customize your polyglot pirate.
