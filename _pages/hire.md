Need help with your Godot game, or with Godot Rapier, or with setting up your game server? I offer **consulting sessions** — short-term, focused help to get you unstuck or point you in the right direction. This is targeted advice and hands-on guidance.

## What I Can Help With

- **Rapier Physics** — Integration, configuration, and troubleshooting of [Godot Rapier Physics](https://github.com/appsinacup/godot-rapier-physics) in your project
- **Godot Game Development** — Architecture, performance optimization, GDExtension development, and general Godot guidance
- **Game Server Development** — Multiplayer backend setup, networking, server infrastructure, and scaling
- **CI/CD & DevOps for Games** — Automated builds, exports, and deployment pipelines for Godot projects
